What CLARISSA Does

💬

Natural Language Interface

Ask questions in plain English. "What's the oil recovery after 10 years?" CLARISSA translates to simulation parameters automatically.

⚡

Multi-Simulator Support

Works with OPM Flow (open-source) and commercial simulators. One interface, multiple backends.

🧠

Physics-Aware AI

Hybrid architecture combining LLM reasoning with physics constraints. No hallucinated reservoir properties.

🎤

Voice Input

Hands-free operation. Dictate simulation scenarios while reviewing results. Powered by Whisper.

📊

Visual Results

Interactive charts, 3D reservoir views, and automated reporting. From simulation to presentation in minutes.

🔒

Air-Gapped Ready

Deploy on-premises with local LLMs. Your proprietary data stays within your infrastructure.
